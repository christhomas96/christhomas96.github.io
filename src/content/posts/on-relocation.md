bangalore is not new to me. i've been staying there for work, floating in and out for years. the strange thing about finally committing to it is that it doesn't feel like arrival — it feels like admitting something.

kerala will always be home in the way that places you grew up become home: involuntarily, permanently. kanjirappally especially — small enough that everyone knows everyone's business, green enough that you remember what air is supposed to smell like. but it was never a place i was going to build a life from. not the kind of life i want, anyway.

the practical reasons for bangalore are obvious. the professional network is there. the kind of people i want to think with are there. the weather is only mildly terrible. but when i try to name what i'm actually moving toward, it's something more like *density* — the feeling that things are happening and i might be in the room where they happen.

maybe that's a fantasy. maybe the room is just another room. but i think you have to bet on the version of your life where it isn't.
